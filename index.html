Settings & rules
  • Total maal = maal of every seen player added together. Unseen players count as 0.
  • Each player's result = (their maal × number of players) − total maal.
  • Every other player then pays the winner a fee: the seen fee if seen, the unseen fee if not, or the Dublee fee if the winner finished with Dublee.
  • A player marked Dublee who doesn't win counts as seen, keeps their maal, and pays the Dublee player fee (0 by default) instead of the seen fee.
  • Players marked Out sit that round out: they aren't counted in the number of players, pay nothing and get nothing. New players can join any time and start from 0.
  • Results across the table always add up to zero. Change the fees above to match your house rules.
